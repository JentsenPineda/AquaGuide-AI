AquaGuide AI provides a way to request deletion of your account and associated application data. Find the available options and learn what happens when your deletion request is processed.

🔒 Account deletion information

How to Delete Your Account

If you can still access AquaGuide AI, you can initiate account deletion directly in the mobile application.

!
Already have the app installed?

Open AquaGuide AI and navigate to the following location:

Profile → Account → Delete Account

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the application, or prefer to request deletion through the web, contact us using the email address below.

Include the email address associated with your AquaGuide AI account so that we can identify the account and process the request. We may ask for additional verification to protect your account.

✉ Request Account Deletion

This opens your device's or browser's default email application. Sending the email does not itself confirm that deletion has been completed.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

After the request is verified and processed, AquaGuide AI will delete the Firebase Authentication account and associated application data controlled by AquaGuide AI, including:

  • ✓User profile information
  • ✓Logbook entries
  • ✓Reminders
  • ✓AI scan history
  • ✓Daily AI scan usage records
!
Permanent action

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Make sure you no longer need the account data before confirming deletion.

How the Request Is Processed

  1. Submit a deletion request through the in-app Delete Account feature or the email address provided on this page.
  2. Provide the email address associated with your AquaGuide AI account when requesting deletion by email.
  3. We may verify the request to help prevent unauthorized account deletion.
  4. Once verified and processed, the associated account and application data controlled by AquaGuide AI will be deleted.

If you submit your request by email, we will need to review and process it. Sending a request does not mean the account has already been deleted.

Third-Party Services and Data Retention

AquaGuide AI uses third-party services, including Firebase and NaraRouter with its configured AI model provider, to support application features.

Deletion of your AquaGuide AI account and application data does not necessarily delete records independently held by third-party service providers. Those providers may retain information according to their own applicable privacy policies, terms, and retention practices.

For more information about information handling, please read our Privacy Policy.
