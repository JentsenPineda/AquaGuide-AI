Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AquaGuide AI handles information when you use our ornamental fish management mobile application and related services. Please read it carefully.

1. Overview

AquaGuide AI is a mobile application designed to help users explore ornamental fish species and access fishkeeping information and tools. Features may include AI-assisted fish image recognition, a species library, tank and care guidance, disease and breeding information, aquatic plant and equipment information, reminders, and a logbook.

This policy applies to the AquaGuide AI app and the services it uses. Some features may process information differently depending on whether you use them, whether you sign in, and the permissions you grant.

2. Information that may be involved

The information involved depends on the features you use. It may include:

  • Account information: information associated with your sign-in account, such as your email address and authentication identifiers, when you create an account or sign in.
  • Fish images: an image you choose or capture and submit for AI-assisted fish recognition.
  • User-created records: reminders, logbook entries, notes, and related information you choose to save in the app.
  • Device permissions: camera or photo-library access when you choose to capture or select an image. You can manage these permissions in your device settings.
  • Technical information: limited information that may be processed by the app platform or service providers to operate, protect, and troubleshoot their services, according to their configurations and policies.

AquaGuide AI does not need you to submit unrelated personal information to browse general species and care content. Please avoid including sensitive or identifying information in images, notes, or other submissions unless it is necessary for your use of the app.

3. AI-assisted fish recognition

If you use the AI fish recognition feature, the image you submit and the prompt or request needed to process it may be sent to a third-party AI service through the application's AI routing provider. The service processes the request and returns a response that the app uses to present an identification or related information.

Important: Do not assume that an image is immediately deleted or that zero data retention applies unless the current terms for the exact provider, model, and service route confirm it. Processing, logging, and retention may depend on the providers and settings in effect when a request is made.

AI results may be inaccurate or incomplete and should be treated as guidance, not a guarantee of species identity, diagnosis, or veterinary advice. Check important fish-care decisions against reliable sources or a qualified aquatic animal professional.

By choosing to use AI recognition, you understand that the image and request must be transmitted to the relevant service provider for processing. If you do not want an image sent to an external AI service, do not use that feature.

4. Authentication and app data services

AquaGuide AI uses Firebase services for account authentication and cloud data features. When you sign in or use cloud-synced features, information necessary for authentication and the data you choose to save may be processed by Firebase according to the app's implementation and Google's applicable terms and privacy documentation.

Reminder and logbook information may be associated with your account so that it can be retrieved across supported sessions or devices. Guest access may be available for some features, while saving certain personal records may require an account.

For more information about Google's handling of information, review the Google Privacy Policy.

5. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • provide account sign-in and account-related functions;
  • process images submitted for AI-assisted recognition and return results;
  • save and display reminders, logbook entries, and other records you create;
  • operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and help protect the app and its services; and
  • respond to support, privacy, or account-deletion requests.

The exact processing depends on the feature used and the service providers involved.

6. Sharing and third-party services

AquaGuide AI does not operate in isolation. Information may be transmitted to service providers when needed to deliver a feature, including:

  • AI routing and model providers: images and associated requests submitted for AI recognition are sent for processing.
  • Firebase / Google: authentication and cloud-data services used by the app may process account and saved app information.
  • Your device and operating system: camera, photo selection, and local notification features operate through platform capabilities when you grant permission.

These providers may handle information under their own terms, privacy policies, technical settings, and retention practices. Review the applicable provider documentation for details. This policy does not override third-party policies.

7. Storage and retention

Account and cloud-saved records may remain stored while needed to provide the relevant app features, subject to the service configuration and applicable provider practices. Information sent to third-party AI services may be processed or retained according to the policies and settings applicable to the specific service route.

We do not make a blanket promise that all submitted images or requests are immediately erased. If you need to know the precise retention period for a specific AI provider or model, consult that provider's current documentation or contact us for information about the route used by the app.

8. Security

Reasonable measures and the security features of the underlying platforms are used to support the operation of the app. However, no app, internet transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your account credentials private and use the device permissions and account controls available to you.

9. Your choices and account deletion

  • You can choose whether to use AI recognition and whether to submit an image.
  • You can manage camera and photo-library permissions through your device settings.
  • You can choose what information to enter into reminders and logbook notes.
  • You can request account deletion using the instructions on our Account & Data Deletion page.

Deleting an account may not instantly remove information held independently by third-party providers or records they are required or permitted to retain. Any deletion request will be handled in accordance with the app's capabilities and applicable provider processes.

10. Children's privacy

AquaGuide AI is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the concern and take appropriate steps where possible.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the app, its features, or its service providers change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date. Please review it periodically.
