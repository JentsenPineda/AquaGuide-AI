A mobile application designed to help ornamental fish keepers understand, manage, and care for their aquatic pets.

About AquaGuide AI

AquaGuide AI is an ornamental fish management mobile application that provides practical information and guidance for fish keepers. The application brings fish care information and management tools together in one place.

🐠 Species LibraryExplore information about ornamental fish species.
🔍 Fish RecognitionUse the fish scanning feature to help identify ornamental fish.
💧 Fish CareAccess tank, water, and general care guidance.
🩺 Disease GuideLearn about common fish health problems and related care information.
🌱 Aqua PlantsExplore information about aquatic plants used in fishkeeping.
🔔 Reminders & LogbookKeep track of fishkeeping activities, reminders, and records.

Privacy & Data

AquaGuide AI is committed to providing users with clear information about how their data is handled. Users can manage their account and delete their account and associated application data.

Account Deletion

Users who have an AquaGuide AI account can delete it directly from the application through Profile → Account → Delete Account. Users who no longer have access to the application can also use the account deletion information provided on our website.
